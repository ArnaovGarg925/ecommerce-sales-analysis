# Business Insights Report — E-Commerce Sales Analysis

**Prepared by:** ARNAOV GARG
**Dataset:** Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset (Kaggle)
**Period covered:** Sep 2016 – Aug 2018 (delivered orders)

---

### 1. Health & Beauty is the top revenue-generating category — worth doubling down on
Health & Beauty brought in roughly **R$ 1.41 million**, the highest of any category, narrowly
ahead of Watches & Gifts and Bed, Bath & Table (see Chart 1 — Revenue by Category). These top
three categories together account for a large share of total revenue, while the tail of
categories contributes very little each.
**Recommendation:** Prioritise marketing spend, homepage placement, and inventory investment on
the top 3–4 categories, since they're already proven demand drivers.

### 2. Sales peaked sharply in November 2017 — plan ahead for seasonal spikes
Monthly revenue grew steadily through 2017 before spiking to its highest point in
**November 2017 (~R$ 1.15 million)**, coinciding with the Black Friday shopping period
(see Chart 2 — Monthly Sales Trend). Sales dipped slightly the following month before
stabilising at a higher baseline through 2018.
**Recommendation:** Treat November as a planned peak — increase stock levels, seller
onboarding, and delivery capacity in the weeks leading up to it rather than reacting after
demand has already spiked.

### 3. São Paulo (SP) dominates regional sales — other states are an untapped opportunity
SP alone generated **R$ 5.77 million**, roughly triple the next-highest state (Rio de Janeiro),
and the gap widens further down the list (see Chart 3 — Regional Sales). Sales are heavily
concentrated in Brazil's southeast.
**Recommendation:** Investigate why states outside the SP–RJ–MG corridor underperform —
likely candidates are delivery time/cost and lower brand awareness. A targeted regional
marketing push or lower shipping thresholds in mid-tier states could unlock new revenue.

### 4. Average order value is moderate and fairly stable — there's room to increase it
The overall average order value is **R$ 159.83** (see Chart 4 — Order Value Distribution),
with most orders clustered in a relatively narrow band and a long tail of higher-value orders.
This suggests customers are mostly making single, planned purchases rather than large basket orders.
**Recommendation:** Introduce cross-sell bundles or a free-shipping threshold just above the
current AOV to nudge customers toward slightly larger baskets.

### 5. Customer satisfaction is generally strong, but a meaningful minority are unhappy
The average review score is **4.09 / 5**, and **57.8%** of reviews are 5-star — a healthy
majority. However, **11.5%** of reviews are 1-star, which is a large enough group to hurt repeat
purchase rates if left unaddressed (see Chart 5 — Review Score Distribution).
**Recommendation:** Pull a sample of 1-star reviews and cross-reference with delivery delays or
specific categories/sellers to find the root cause, then fix the top 1–2 causes first for the
biggest impact.
